Model state

Counted votes are always held fixed -- the model never revises a vote that's actually been tallied. What it doesn't take on faith is the RATE for whatever hasn't been counted yet: a county's uncounted remainder is projected from a credibility-weighted blend of that county's own results so far and the pre-election baseline, shifting further toward observed results as more of the county reports, and faster still when many counties show the same pattern, not just one.
